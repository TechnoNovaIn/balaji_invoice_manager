Seamless Free Quota Multi-Key Failover

Google Gemini free tier grants 1,500 requests/day per API project key. By configuring a pool of 5 to 6 keys, Balaji InvoiceHub achieves 7,500 to 9,000 free extractions/day.

  • Zero-Error Failover: When Key 1 hits the rate limit (HTTP 429), the engine automatically switches to Key 2 with zero errors for invoices.
  • Thread-Safe Cooldown: Exhausted keys enter a cooldown period and recover automatically without blocking new bills.
  • Generate Keys: Create free projects at Google AI Studio ↗.
